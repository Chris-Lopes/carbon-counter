Impactful NGOs in India

Educate for Tomorrow

Bangalore, Karnataka

Providing quality education to underprivileged children in rural India.

Impact: Established 100 schools and educated over 50,000 children.

Green Earth Foundation

Mumbai, Maharashtra

Promoting environmental conservation and sustainable living practices across India.

Impact: Planted over 1 million trees and educated 500,000 people on sustainable living.

Health for All Initiative

New Delhi, Delhi

Improving healthcare access in remote areas of India through mobile clinics.

Impact: Provided medical care to over 200,000 patients in 500 villages.

Women Empowerment League

Jaipur, Rajasthan

Empowering women through skill development and entrepreneurship programs.

Impact: Trained 75,000 women and helped establish 5,000 small businesses.
